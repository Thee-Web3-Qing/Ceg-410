Structural analysis theory

A two-dimensional, pin-jointed truss carries axial forces through straight members. The matrix displacement method assembles each member stiffness into the global matrix, applies support constraints and solves for unknown nodal displacements.

Governing equations

Kff × uf = Ff determines free displacements.

R = K × u − F determines reactions.

N = EA/L × [−c, −s, c, s] × ue determines member axial force.

This is linear-elastic, small-displacement truss analysis. Beam bending and frame analysis are not yet implemented.
